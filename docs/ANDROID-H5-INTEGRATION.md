# Android H5 purchase integration

The original H5 remains unchanged. It sends `pay` through the provider WebSocket and, on `purchased`, refreshes its profile using the provider's `/app-api/member/user/get`. Inspection of the currently served H5 source on 2026-11-05 showed that it determines its displayed membership from `vipType` / `memberVipType`, not from the app's `subscription.getStatus` message. Its playback quota store does not currently consult that membership state. This observation is a source inspection, not evidence that payment or membership works in production.

The Android client uses Google Play for monthly/yearly subscriptions, saves an account/profile association before purchase, and sends a locally verified matching purchase token to the original provider's `POST /app-api/flow/rights-order/google/buy`. That endpoint appears in the provider's current OpenAPI document as “google pay 购买权益”. The documented request has `rightsName`, `price`, `usdPrice`, `purchaseToken`, `packageName`, `versionCode`, `payType`, `source` and `all`. The order response schema describes pay type `2` as Google. The client sends pay type `2`, source `android`, product ID as `rightsName`, the Play price converted from micros to hundredths as `price`, and `usdPrice` only when Play reports USD. It omits the undocumented `all` field. The meaning of `source`, `all`, currency handling, the response string and product configuration remain unconfirmed for this provider.

The client reports `purchased` only after a non-empty successful provider response, a refreshed provider profile that both has H5's membership type and a future expiry later than the previously active expiry, and Google Play acknowledgement. If a request to the Google purchase endpoint has an uncertain outcome, the original purchase is retained and the app will not submit that token a second time automatically: the Google endpoint's idempotency and validation/fulfillment behavior are not documented. A confirmed provider response can be checked again without re-submitting. Unknown/other-user purchase records remain unfinished. Restore requires a current local B-side attribution record, a locally verified active Play subscription, and an active provider profile; reinstall/device migration may lose the local record, just as in the reference app.

A Google Play app, the chosen `satchel_monthly` and `satchel_yearly` subscription products and auto-renewing base plans, and the app's Play licensing public key (`SATCHEL_PLAY_PUBLIC_KEY`) are still required. Provider-side Google Play API credentials, production endpoints, product mapping, idempotency, renewal/refund handling and the actual H5 membership/benefit rules must be confirmed through an end-to-end test when the Play account is available. The source code and OpenAPI document alone cannot establish that the endpoint really verifies the token or grants the expected rights. No real payment, entitlement, WebSocket or ad validation was performed.

The optional `server/` prototype remains on disk from earlier work, but the Android app does not use it. The original H5 and provider are not modified.
